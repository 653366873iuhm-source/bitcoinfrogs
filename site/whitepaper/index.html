1. What Bitcoin Frogs is

Bitcoin Frogs ($Frogs) is a meme token on BNB Smart Chain with a fair, on-chain mint reserved for AI agents: Binance Agentic Wallets holding a .ai Agent ID. Supply only comes into existence when shares are minted. No presale, no private round, no team allocation. Ground operations are run by the Pangu Community ground team in ground-push mode.

2. Token

  • Name / symbol: Bitcoin Frogs / Frogs, 18 decimals, BEP-20. Token 0x885417732c10F5EcA16c70e28700A19Cb39029ad.
  • Hard cap: 10,000,000,000,000,000 Frogs (10,000T), enforced by the token. Only the mint contract can create tokens.
  • Buy tax 1% (converted to BNB for the treasury). Sell tax 1% burned + 1% converted to BNB for the treasury. Wallet-to-wallet transfers: no tax. Rates and recipient are fixed in code.
  • Until the pool opens, Frogs cannot be transferred. The pool opens automatically in the transaction that mints share 10,000, and the transfer lock is lifted in that same transaction. Afterwards only the official Frogs/USDT PancakeSwap pair 0x955c52F9bf850792709FCB5b7c6c776E67EB2B13 is accepted as a pool: transfers into other pairs holding Frogs, the PancakeSwap V4 PoolManager and the Infinity Vault are refused. Routers can never be blocked.

3. Minting

  • Mint contract FrogsMint 0xd9e0b22b91cE1DE440c7B4FbF8a4Cb361a210131. 100,000,000 shares in total. Minting opens at SALE_START (2026-10-31 21:30 CST).
  • No pause: the contract has no pause function and no operator. Once the sale starts, nobody, including the team, can pause or stop minting.
  • Eligibility: the sending wallet must hold a .ai Agent ID (read on-chain from the SPACE ID registry).
  • Shares #1–#2,000: free, one per Agent ID (claimFree()), gas only.
  • Shares #2,001–#7,000: 1 USDT each (5,000 shares). Shares #7,001–#10,000: 2 USDT each (3,000 shares).
  • Every share up to #10,000 mints 50,000,000 Frogs to the minter and parks 55,000,000 Frogs in the contract for the pool.
  • From #10,001 the price table continues: 1.10 USDT, +0.10 every 1,000 to 2.00; 20,001–100,000: 2.10, +0.02 every 1,000; 100,001–1,000,000: 3.70, +0.02 every 10,000; then 5.50, +0.015 every 20,000 (rounded to the cent) up to 79.74 at 100,000,000. 5,132 tiers; all paid shares together 4,223,768,700 USDT at table prices. From #10,001 the payment buys Frogs at the pool price minus 1%.
  • Paid quota: 10 shares per Agent ID, +1 per invited Agent ID's first paid mint, +1 per social task. Max 10 shares per transaction. The free share is outside this quota.
  • Pay with USDT (approve, then mint with a maximum cost) or with BNB (priced with Chainlink BNB/USD plus 1%, swapped to USDT on PancakeSwap in the same transaction; BNB above the quote is refunded).

4. Liquidity

  • Before share #10,000, 100% of every payment stays in the mint contract for the pool; nothing goes to the treasury.
  • The transaction that reaches share #10,000 opens the official Frogs/USDT PancakeSwap pool automatically with everything parked: 11,000 USDT (5,000 × 1 + 3,000 × 2) and 550,000,000,000 Frogs (10,000 × 55M). The LP tokens go to the dead address: they are burned and nobody can remove this liquidity.
  • After the pool opens, 100% of each later mint payment goes to the treasury (0xD02eF85B1b022B2Bd32f4E7D7EdcD123e2B91375). Mints no longer add liquidity. For a BNB payment, any USDT the swap returns above the table price also goes to the treasury.
  • Floor line: line = total USDT paid × 7,616,685,108 / 4,223,765,700 (≈ 1.80 × total paid). If the pool value of circulating Frogs is below the line, the mint burns Frogs from the pool, raising the price by at most 0.8% per transaction and always keeping at least 11,666,666,667 Frogs in the pool.
  • Collected tax tokens are converted to BNB for the treasury at most once per block and at most 0.10% of the pool's Frogs reserve per conversion.
  • Minting ends at share 100,000,000 or at the 10,000T supply cap.

5. 30-day rule

If share #10,000 is not reached within 30 days of the sale start (deadline 2026-11-30 21:30 CST), the pool does not open. Only the project wallet (treasury) can then withdraw the parked USDT (the parked Frogs go to the dead address), and the project refunds buyers manually. The refund is a promise by the project and is not enforced by the contract.

6. Independence

Bitcoin Frogs is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Binance, SPACE ID, PancakeSwap or Chainlink; it only uses their public on-chain contracts. Not audited. No promise of profit.
